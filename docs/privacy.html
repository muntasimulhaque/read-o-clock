Read-o-Clock: Privacy Policy

Read-o-Clock does not collect, share, or transmit any data. Ever.

Read-o-Clock is a paid, offline wall clock for teaching a child to read the time. It has no advertising, no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, and no internet permission at all. The app cannot connect to the network, because the Android system gives it no way to.

What stays on your device

Nothing is stored at all: not a setting, not a preference, not the time you last set. The clock keeps the time in memory only while it is open. Closing and reopening the app starts from the real local time again, and uninstalling it leaves nothing behind.

What we never do

  • We never collect personal data of any kind, from anyone.
  • We never show ads or promote other products.
  • We never send anything over the internet.
  • We never ask a child (or a parent) for a name, an email, an age, or a location.

Children

Read-o-Clock is designed for young children and complies with Google Play's Families policy. Because it collects nothing, transmits nothing, and shows nothing but a clock, it is safe by construction, not just by policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date. Given how the app is built, it is hard to imagine what could change.
